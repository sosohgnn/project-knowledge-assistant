The group discussed the recommendation. Marcus Bell asked what it would mean for costs. Elena Marsh asked to be told directly if Bristol's timing changes. Priya Shah said she would want to see the impact on dates and cost before considering it.

**No decision was made.** The Bristol rollout approach in the project brief is unchanged.
